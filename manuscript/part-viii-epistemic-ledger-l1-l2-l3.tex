\subsection{Epistemic Ledger: Strict Demarcation of Open Residues}
\label{sec:epistemic_ledger}

\noindent This subsection constitutes the formal Epistemic Ledger of
\UIDT\ v3.9. Every entry is a hard boundary, not a programme aspiration.
Evidence tags are mandatory and non-negotiable; no claim may be presented
at a higher stratum than its tag permits. The three limitations below are
the load-bearing open residues of the framework as of the present
canonical edition.

% ------------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph{Limitation L1: Dimensional Transmutation and the Origin
  of $\gamma$.}
\textbf{Status:~[A-] (Internally Calibrated) / Tension Entry~[C].}

The interface weight $\gamma = 16.339$ is phenomenologically calibrated
and its classification is~\textbf{[A-] permanent}; it is not~[A] and
may not be described as \emph{derived}.\Aminus\ The underlying
topological generator is identified via the SU(3) Casimir structure
\begin{equation}
  \gamma_{\text{bare}}
  = \frac{(2N_c+1)^2}{N_c}\bigg|_{N_c=3}
  = \frac{49}{3}
  \approx 16.333,
  \label{eq:gamma-bare}
\end{equation}
which provides the bare algebraic value.\Aminus\ The residual discrepancy
\begin{equation}
  \Delta\gamma = \gamma - \gamma_{\text{bare}}
  = 16.339 - 16.333 = +0.00567
  \label{eq:delta-gamma}
\end{equation}
is formally registered in the ledger as an \emph{open tension} between
the pure algebraic topology and the empirically calibrated readout at the
observer interface.\Cminus\ A Monte-Carlo internal determination
$\gamma_{\text{MC}}$ is held alongside $16.339$ as a separate recorded
tension; the two determinations must be displayed with their methods and
uncertainties and are not to be reconciled by selection.\Aminus

In strict adherence to the framework's \textbf{Anti-Target-Leakage
Protocol} (Proposition~\ref{prop:target-leakage}): no ab-initio
derivation of $\gamma = 16.339$ is attempted from within any solver that
has access to this value. Such imports constitute tautological fitting
rather than physical derivation and are prohibited.\Aminus\ Any future
derivation must satisfy, in advance and in public: (i)~the operator
whose flow it integrates, (ii)~the flow equation, (iii)~the regulator,
(iv)~boundary conditions, (v)~an explicit proof that no target value has
leaked into the computation, and (vi)~an executable reproduction
command. Absent any one of these conditions, a claimed derivation of
$\gamma$ remains at predictive status~[D] at best.\Aminus

\paragraph{Limitation L2: Yukawa Couplings and the Relocation
  Necessity Conjecture.}
\textbf{Status:~[D] (Conjecture\,/\,Formal Hypothesis) /
  [E] (Systemic Concealment).}

The NCG structure enforces the Standard Model algebra
$\mathbb{C} \oplus \mathbb{H} \oplus M_3(\mathbb{C})$ as the
topologically unique surviving partition under entropy-penalty
competition in Matrix Thermodynamics~[D].\Dclass\ Specifically, the free
energy functional
\begin{equation}
  F = U - TS, \quad
  S = \textstyle\sum_i n_i^2, \quad
  U_{\text{off}} \propto \textstyle\sum_{i<j} n_i n_j \, V_{ij},
  \label{eq:free-energy}
\end{equation}
subject to two topological filters (intersection-form non-degeneracy and
mass-degeneration instability), eliminates all integer partitions of
$N=6$ except $(3,2,1)$.\Dclass\ This case-by-case elimination for $N=6$
is documented; the claim that $(3,2,1)$ is the unique global minimum for
all $N$ is registered as the \textbf{Relocation Necessity Conjecture
(RNC)} at status~\textbf{[D]} and constitutes the primary open
mathematical deliverable of this research programme.\Dclass

However, the granular Yukawa couplings — fermion masses and their
hierarchies — remain \emph{free parameters} of the finite Dirac operator
$D_F$. The NCG construction constrains the algebra but does not dictate
the scaling of these parameters.\Dclass\ Due to the principle of
\textbf{computational irreducibility}, the precise algorithmic generation
of these infrared weights is systemically concealed from the
$(3+1)$D observer interface; no derivation from within the framework's
effective field theory domain is possible in principle.\Eclass

Any prior generation-scaling rules (e.g.\ mass-ratio formulae involving
factors of $100 \times \Delta$ or geometric harmonics $\sqrt{9/\gamma}$)
are \textbf{strictly demoted} to post-dictive mappings~\textbf{[C/D]}
and carry no status of formal derivation. These mappings are preserved in
the museum record as documentation of a failed approach; they are not
admissible as live calculations.\Aminus

\paragraph{Limitation L3: UV-Completion and the $d^2\!=\!0$
  Obstruction.}
\textbf{Status:~[A] (Effective Field Theory Demarcation).}

The physical domain of the Forgetful Functor is strictly bounded by the
Information Saturation Bound $\rho_{\max} = 4 \times 10^{99}$, which
constitutes the final kill-switch of the effective theory.\Aclass\ The
inability of the scalar field $S$ to generate unmediated UV curvature is
mathematically formalised via the \textbf{$d^2=0$ obstruction}: for any
real scalar $S$,
\begin{equation}
  F = d^2 S \cdot dS = 0 \quad \text{identically},
  \label{eq:d2-obstruction}
\end{equation}
which establishes that $S$ alone cannot generate Yang-Mills curvature
below or above the EFT cutoff $\Lambda$.\Aclass\ This is Limitation~L7
in its mathematical hardware form and closes the scalar-gradient route to
gauge structure as a~[A] negative result.\Aclass

The unreduced absolute state $\mathcal{M}_{\text{UV}}$ beyond
$\rho_{\max}$ admits no physical history, no fluctuation, and no
cosmological epoch; all singular genesis narratives are excluded as
category errors.\Aclass\ A non-perturbative UV completion remains an
open research question at status~[D]; the present effective Lagrangian
(Equation~\ref{eq:lagrangian}) is explicitly not renormalisable by power
counting (dimension-5 operator), and this limitation is openly
recorded.\Aclass
